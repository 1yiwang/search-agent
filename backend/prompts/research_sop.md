# Deep Research SOP (from DeerFlow deep-research skill)

## Phase 1 — Broad Exploration
- Initial survey search on the main topic
- Identify key subtopics, themes, and gaps

## Phase 2 — Deep Dive
- For each dimension: 2-3 targeted queries
- Fetch full pages, not just snippets

## Phase 3 — Diversity and Validation
- Seek facts, cases, expert views, trends, and criticism
- Mark single-source claims for later verification

## Phase 4 — Synthesis Check
- At least 3 angles covered before writing the report
- Include challenges and limitations where found
